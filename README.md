## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Eros Memories: The Lewd Way to Save the World**. This trainer assists with daily-life progression, character development, Divine Sight events, clue collection, minigames, and route management. ## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

The game uses a modern daily-life structure rather than an action-focused movement system. Progress through available locations, conversations, activities, and story prompts. Keep progression options disabled while transitioning between scenes if the game becomes unstable.

### Tools & Inventory

The game emphasizes clues, story progression, character development, and interactive activities instead of a large equipment-based inventory. Use inventory-related trainer options only when an item or clue is visibly present in the current session.

### World & Environment

Explore the modern setting, follow phone news and NPC conversations, and investigate the mystery surrounding Mist Island, the ancient charm, the black cat deity, and the protagonist's hidden power. Collect clues carefully because the hidden ending requires broader investigation.

### Combat & Weapons

Eros Memories does not center on conventional combat. Its main challenges come from choices, daily-life scheduling, relationship progression, puzzles, Divine Sight discoveries, and minigames. The trainer therefore focuses on progression and story assistance rather than health, damage, or ammunition enhancements.
